Contrast Reference

Metadata Contrast Requirements

To meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) AA standards, text must have a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against its background. This ensures legibility for users with visual impairments. Often, supplementary text like metadata (dates, statuses) is styled too lightly, causing it to fail these essential checks.

Ensure all text, including metadata and labels, meets the 4.5:1 ratio against solid backgrounds.
